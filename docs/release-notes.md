**Spotify Premium required.** Windows 10 / 11, 64-bit.

On first launch, SpotiLite walks you through creating your Spotify app (2 minutes, once only).
The full guide is on the [project page](https://github.com/Clemslegoat/SpotiLite#readme).

If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC": *More info* → *Run anyway* (the executable is not
signed).
